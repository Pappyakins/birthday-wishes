# Birthday Wishes

Never miss a celebration. A mobile-first, no-account, no-server birthday companion by JakinsCraft.

- **Upcoming** — birthdays sorted by what's next, with "today / tomorrow / in N days" chips and age countdowns
- **Celebrate** — one-tap heartfelt wish composer (24 templates + optional Scripture verse + your sign-off), share straight to WhatsApp
- **Card** — generate a beautiful navy & gold birthday card with the celebrant's name, download or share it
- **People** — keep your list on your device, import/export CSV, add by hand

Your data never leaves your phone. No account, no server.

Live: https://pappyakins.github.io/birthday-wishes/
